FAQS

Common questions

1 Do I need to book in advance, or are walk-ins welcome?

Walk-in guests are always warmly received at both our Chennai (Besant Nagar) and Mysore (Gokulam) sanctuaries. However, reserving ahead ensures your preferred private therapy suite and master therapist are held exclusively for your schedule.

2 How long does each session take and what is included?

Session intervals range from 30 to 90 minutes depending on your chosen restoration ritual. Express rituals (30–45 min) focus on deep reflex zones from knee to toes, while Premium & Signature rituals (60–90 min) seamlessly weave full foot reflexology with neck, shoulder, and back therapy.

Every session includes pure organic botanical compresses, therapeutic foot bath preparation, and personalized acoustic suite ambiance.

3 How does foot reflexology relieve chronic back and neck pain?

The human foot contains over 7,000 nerve endings connecting directly to the central nervous and kinetic systems. Precision manual pressure along the medial long arch and calcaneus decompresses tension along the spinal column, relieving chronic lumbar, shoulder, and cervical strain.

4 What should I wear to my reflexology and bodywork session?

We recommend loose, comfortable clothing. For foot reflexology, your therapist will work up to your knees; comfortable trousers or joggers are ideal. We provide soft cotton spa slippers, comfortable recliners, and fresh linens for all rituals.

5 Do you accommodate group bookings or private sanctuary sessions?

Yes. We accommodate private wellness sessions and group reservations upon prior appointment at either our Besant Nagar (Chennai) or Gokulam (Mysore) sanctuary.
